The Story So Far...

Once upon time in the land of Portland Oregon, there was skinny teenage boy with thick long hair who wanted a girlfriend. Unfortunately that wasn’t working out so he started writing songs instead.

One day in the middle of calculus class, he impulsively decided to kill his old self and shave his long beautiful hair off. On that day he was reborn as The Cosplay Jesus.

Now Cosplay Jesus has embarked on a holy war with his demons in pursuit of international rockstardom. Failure is not an option.
